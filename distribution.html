Combined Box Plot & Violin Plot - Weekly CFR Distribution (Top 10 European Countries)

📊 Understanding Case Fatality Rate (CFR)

Case Fatality Rate (CFR) is calculated as: (Weekly Deaths / Weekly Cases) × 100

This visualization shows the distribution of weekly CFR across the top 10 European countries by total cases. The combined plot reveals:

  • Violin Plot (Teal): Shows the full distribution shape using kernel density estimation. Wider sections indicate higher frequency of CFR values at that level.
  • Box Plot (Purple): Overlaid on the violin, shows the five-number summary (minimum, Q1, median, Q3, maximum). The box represents the middle 50% of data (Q1 to Q3).
  • Yellow line: Median CFR (middle value when sorted)
  • Red dashed line: 1% CFR reference line
  • Data filtering: Only weeks with valid cases and deaths are included, with CFR capped at 10% for visualization clarity

💡 Hover over the plots to see detailed CFR statistics for each country.
